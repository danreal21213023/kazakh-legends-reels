# English Explained — Series Bible (v1)

**Series name:** English, explained · Ағылшын тілі — қарапайым
**Days:** Wednesday and Friday · **Format:** Instagram Reel, 1080×1920, 30 fps, 25–40 s
**Promise:** one common mistake fixed in under 40 seconds, by a real English teacher.

## Look: "the teacher's notebook"
It must look clearly different from the paper-cutout legends.
- **World:** a page of a squared exercise book (5 mm grid) seen from slightly above, lying on a desk. The camera moves over the page like a hand-held phone: gentle drift, push-ins on the important word, a fast whip between "wrong" and "right".
- **The red pen is the character.** Mistakes get crossed out with a hand-drawn red stroke (animated on), corrections get written in with Caveat handwriting, and the right answer gets a ✓ tick and a yellow highlighter swipe.
- **Supporting objects (flat vector, soft shadows):** sticky notes for examples, a pencil, a paper clip, a teacher's stamp "✓ CORRECT" for the final reveal. These objects have weight: sticky notes flap when they land, and the stamp lands with a squash.
- **Palette:** paper #FBF8F1 · grid #D9E2EC · ink navy #1D2B53 · red pen #E63946 · highlighter #FFE066 · mint (correct) #2EC4B6 · desk #C9A27E
- **Fonts:** Rubik 800 for headlines and English examples; **Caveat 700** for red-pen notes and Kazakh hints; Montserrat 800 for the quiz. All three are checked for ә ғ қ ң ө ұ ү һ і.
- **Motion rules:** text is never just faded in. It gets written on stroke by stroke, stamped, slid in on a sticky note, or typed with a caret. There's a new visual beat every 2–3 s.

## Fixed structure (every episode)
1. **Hook (0–2 s):** the WRONG sentence big on the page, a red question mark, and a line like "Do you say this? ✗". No intro, no logo.
2. **Cross-out (2–5 s):** the red pen strikes the mistake, with a pen-scratch sound.
3. **Why (5–12 s):** one short reason in plain English, plus a Kazakh hint in Caveat (e.g. «agree — етістік»).
4. **Right way (12–20 s):** the correct sentence written in, with a highlighter swipe and a ✓ stamp.
5. **Two examples (20–30 s):** two sticky notes with natural sentences.
6. **Quiz (30–36 s):** "Which is right? A / B". The answer is NOT shown, so viewers comment. On-screen: "Answer in the comments 👇".
7. **End plate (last 1.5 s):** the series name and "Save it ✓".

## Sound
Light lo-fi/pizzicato groove at about 96–104 BPM, generated in code (plucks, soft kick, brushed snare, a marimba motif). SFX: pen scratch, paper slide, sticky-note slap, stamp thump, a "ding" on ✓, and a soft buzzer on ✗. Accents land on the cross-out, the ✓ stamp and the quiz reveal. Same loudness target as the legends series (about −14 LUFS).

## Accuracy
Every rule and example is checked against at least two of: Cambridge Dictionary / Cambridge Grammar, Oxford Learner's Dictionaries, British Council LearnEnglish, Merriam-Webster. If sources differ (UK vs US), say so on screen or pick neutral examples. Kazakh hints must be simple and correct; if unsure, leave the Kazakh hint out.
