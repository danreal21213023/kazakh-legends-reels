# English through Kazakh Legends — Series Bible (draft v1)

**Series name:** English through Kazakh Legends · Аңыздар арқылы ағылшын тілі
**Format:** Instagram Reel, 1080×1920, 30 fps, 30–40 s
**Promise to the viewer:** one short legend + 4 English words with Kazakh translations

## Look
- **Style:** cut-paper animation. Layered paper with rough cut edges, soft drop shadows and visible grain. Characters move in "stop-motion" steps (12 poses per second) while the camera glides smoothly at 30 fps.
- **World:** the steppe. Far mountains, rolling hills, a small yurt, feather grass in the foreground (blurred).
- **Palette:** night indigo #1E2350 · dusk violet #4B3A78 · felt red #B23A2E · saffron #F2A93B · camel ochre #C8955A · steppe olive #8C8A4A · paper cream #F4E9D4
- **Fonts:** Lora Bold / Italic for story captions and Kazakh words, Montserrat ExtraBold for hooks and English words. Both checked for ә ғ қ ң ө ұ ү һ і.
- **Light:** each legend moves from night or dusk to sunrise. The colour change marks the story's turning point.

## Fixed parts of every episode
1. **Hook (0–3 s):** a "Why…?" question in big type plus a short Kazakh line. No logo, no intro.
2. **Story (3–30 s):** one caption strip at the top (cream torn paper) and a word tag at the bottom (paper luggage tag on a string).
3. **Word tag:** ENGLISH WORD · /pronunciation/ · қазақша
4. **Turning point:** colour change plus a music accent.
5. **End card (last 2–3 s):** "4 NEW WORDS" list, a call to action ("Save it & test yourself"), and the series name in both languages.
6. **Music:** a dombra-like plucked melody in D minor pentatonic, frame drum, about 100 BPM. Paper rustle on scene changes and a "flick" when a word tag appears.
7. **Safe zones:** nothing important within 250 px of the top, 380 px of the bottom or 150 px of the right edge.

## Episode: "Why is there no Year of the Camel?" (Түйе мен тышқан)
Source version: the folk tale «Жыл басына таласқан хайуанаттар» (The Animals Competing for the New Year). The animals argue about who should lead the year. The mouse proposes that whoever sees the new year (the sunrise) first wins. The proud camel agrees, and the mouse climbs onto the camel's hump and sees it first.
**Variant note:** the ending "the camel still rolls in the ash looking for the mouse" is common in retellings but is not in every text. The script says "Some say…" for that reason.
